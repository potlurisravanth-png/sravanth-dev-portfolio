Late fusion: two streams, one head.

Each stream starts from a pre-trained network. R3D-18 (Kinetics-400) reads motion over time, which is where unnatural lip movement shows. ResNet18 (ImageNet) reads the Mel-spectrogram as an image, looking for artifacts in the frequency domain. Their 512-dimension features are concatenated and a fully connected network makes the call.

  1. Video streamR3D-18, pre-trained on Kinetics-400, reads motion over time: 512 features.
  2. Audio streamResNet18, pre-trained on ImageNet, reads the Mel-spectrogram as an image: 512 features.
  3. Fusion headBoth feature vectors are concatenated and a fully connected network outputs real or fake.
