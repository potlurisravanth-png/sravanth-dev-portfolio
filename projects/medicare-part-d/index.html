41.7% of prescribers had a suppressed count.

CMS suppresses small counts to protect patient privacy, and 7,635 Alabama prescribers had at least one claim count withheld. Reading those blanks as zero would understate them, so they were counted and flagged before any analysis, and the count was cross-checked by two independent methods.
