Medicare Part D Spend Analytics — Alabama

Profiled the complete CMS Medicare Part D prescriber dataset for Alabama — 18,322 prescribers, $5.35B in drug spend, 32.5M claims — pulled through the CMS public data API, with suppression counts cross-validated by two independent methods.

  • Found a 15× gap between mean ($291,946) and median ($19,141) prescriber spend — enough to invalidate average-based benchmarking and force median-and-percentile peer comparison instead.
  • Quantified CMS claim-count suppression affecting 41.7% of prescribers (7,635) before analysis, rather than defaulting suppressed values to zero.
  • Surfaced an 85.3% brand share of branded-plus-generic spend, with Nurse Practitioners the largest prescribing group at $1.03B — 19.3% of state spend.

Python LLM Routing MCP Evaluation

Agentic Router

A cross-provider LLM router that picks the cheapest model that still passes — and proves it. Every routed call returns a receipt (tier, models used, real cost), and in-process cost is reconciled against the provider's billing API, failing loudly on drift.

  • Verifier-bounded routing — a cheap model's answer is checked before it is trusted; a failure climbs within the low-cost pool, and anything beyond it is flagged for a frontier model. On 1,095 public benchmark questions the learned route map matched the best single model's accuracy at 54–68% of its cost.
  • Pre-registered evaluation — experiments are reported whether they pass or fail, and new behavior ships switched off until the evidence is in. A methodology paper is in preparation.
  • Agent mode (experimental) — runs as an MCP server for Claude Code, handing repository tasks to cheaper worker models while Claude reviews the result: 25% cheaper than Claude Opus at low effort on eight real tasks, with every hidden test still passing.
