Medicare Part D Spend Analytics — Alabama

Profiled the complete CMS Medicare Part D prescriber dataset for Alabama — 18,322 prescribers, $5.35B in drug spend, 32.5M claims — pulled through the CMS public data API, with suppression counts cross-validated by two independent methods.

  • Found a 15× gap between mean ($291,946) and median ($19,141) prescriber spend — enough to invalidate average-based benchmarking and force median-and-percentile peer comparison instead.
  • Quantified CMS claim-count suppression affecting 41.7% of prescribers (7,635) before analysis, rather than defaulting suppressed values to zero.
  • Surfaced an 85.3% brand share of branded-plus-generic spend, with Nurse Practitioners the largest prescribing group at $1.03B — 19.3% of state spend.
